# Research

## Quick Version

This folder is for background research that supports mission and system decisions.

Keep notes short enough that another teammate can understand why the research matters.

## Details

Useful research topics include:

- AEAC ConOps interpretation
- target detection methods
- payload handling ideas
- telemetry and radio-link notes
- field-test lessons learned

When research becomes an operating procedure, move the final instructions into `planning/mission` or the relevant pipeline README.
