# AEAC 2026 RTM Radio SOP Checklist
*Quick-Reference Operator Card — Printable for the Flight Line*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ATC compliance is worth 15 points in Task 2.**
> - One handheld radio will be provided by ATC. Substitutions are NOT permitted.
> - The radio operator **must NOT** be the pilot-in-command (PIC).
> - All radio calls must use the **ICAO Phonetic Alphabet** for letters and numbers.
> - All headings must be reported in **3-digit degrees magnetic**, clockwise from magnetic north.
> - A heading of north must be reported as **"Three-Six-Zero"**, never "Zero-Zero-Zero".

---

## 1. Registration & Setup

### Callsign Registration
- [ ] Register callsign with Big City in the format: `<aircraft model OR manufacturer> <3 numbers><1 letter>`
  * *Example:* **"Vulcan Four-Zero-Six-Kilo"** (for Vulcan 406K)

### Test Communication (upon receiving radio)
- [ ] **Transmit:** `"[Full Callsign] to base, radio check."`
  * *Example:* *"Vulcan Four-Zero-Six-Kilo to base, radio check."*
- [ ] **Expect reply:** ATC will reply with signal strength and clarity (1 to 5, e.g., *"Five by five"*).

---

## 2. Standard Flight Procedures

### Takeoff Clearance
1. [ ] **Request takeoff:** `"[Full Callsign] to base, request takeoff."`
2. [ ] **Expect reply:** `"Base to [Callsign], you are clear for takeoff."`
3. [ ] **Acknowledge:** `"Cleared to takeoff, [Full Callsign]."`
4. [ ] **Action:** Pilot takes off.
5. [ ] **Report complete:** `"[Full Callsign] takeoff complete."`
   > [!WARNING]
   > If takeoff is delayed by more than **5 minutes** after receiving clearance, transmit:
   > `"[Full Callsign] to base, takeoff delayed."` and restart takeoff procedure.

### UAM Corridor Operations (Corridor Altitude: 20m to 35m)
- [ ] **Corridor Entry:** Enter corridor and stay inside for at least **30 seconds**.
- [ ] **Report entry:** `"[Full Callsign] entering corridor."`
- [ ] **Corridor Exit:** Upon departing the corridor to approach the building:
- [ ] **Report departure:** `"[Full Callsign] has left the corridor."`

### Building Proximity
- [ ] **Report proximity:** `"[Full Callsign] operating near the building."` when entering the search volume around the building.
  * *Note:* This transmission only needs to be made once per flight.

### Landing Clearance
1. [ ] **Request landing:** `"[Full Callsign] to base, request landing."`
2. [ ] **Expect reply:** `"Base to [Callsign], you are clear for landing."`
3. [ ] **Acknowledge:** `"Cleared to land, [Full Callsign]."`
4. [ ] **Action:** Pilot lands.
5. [ ] **Report complete:** `"[Full Callsign] landed."`
   > [!WARNING]
   > If landing is delayed by more than **5 minutes** after receiving clearance, transmit:
   > `"[Full Callsign] to base, landing delayed."` and restart landing procedure.

## 3. Dispatch & Emergency Procedures

### Emergency Dispatch Briefing
1. [ ] **Expect transmission:** `"Dispatch to [Full Callsign], acknowledge."`
2. [ ] **Reply:** `"[Full Callsign] to dispatch, go ahead."`
3. [ ] **Acknowledge briefing receipt:** `"[Full Callsign] Wilco."`
   * *Note:* You may ask the dispatcher to repeat details or ask questions to clarify before transmitting "Wilco".

### Airspace Deconfliction (Traffic Alert)
1. [ ] **Expect transmission:** `"Base to [Callsign], traffic inbound from heading [HDG], altitude [ALT]; correct course."`
2. [ ] **Reply:** `"Correcting course, [Full Callsign]."`
3. [ ] **Heading Correction:** Select a new heading **+/- 90 degrees** from the traffic heading. Fly on new heading for at least **10m**.
4. [ ] **Report heading:** `"Flying heading [NEW_HDG], [Full Callsign]."`
5. [ ] **Altitude Correction:** Select a new altitude at least **15m** away from traffic altitude.
6. [ ] **Report altitude:** `"Climbing/descending to [NEW_ALT], [Full Callsign]."`
7. [ ] **Conclude:** Wait for ATC to transmit: `"Base to [Callsign], traffic avoided."`

### Yield to Medevac (Priority Flight Alert)
1. [ ] **Expect transmission:** `"Base to [Callsign], priority flight inbound, fly [DISTANCE] [DIRECTION], climb to 50m."`
2. [ ] **Reply:** `"Confirm fly [DISTANCE] [DIRECTION], climb to 50m, [Full Callsign]."`
3. [ ] **Action:** Move aircraft to specified distance/direction, climb to 50m.
4. [ ] **Report yielded:** `"[Full Callsign] to base, yielded."`
5. [ ] **Expect reply:** `"Base to [Callsign] affirmative, hold position."`
6. [ ] **Conclude:** Hold position at 50m altitude until ATC transmits: `"Base to [Callsign], continue mission."`

### Ground All (Emergency Grounding)
1. [ ] **Expect transmission:** `"Base to all, land immediately."`
2. [ ] **Action:** Land immediately in a safe place.
3. [ ] **Report landed:** `"[Full Callsign] to ATC, landed."`
4. [ ] **Conclude:** Keep UAV grounded until ATC transmits: `"Base to all, resume flights."`
